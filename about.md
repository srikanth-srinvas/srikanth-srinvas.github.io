---**About me**
<p align="justify">

I'm currently a postgraduate MSc Bioinformatics student at the University of Bristol with experience in Genomics and Genomic Data Analysis. I've gained hands-on bioinformatics experience by interning at a NIHR Global Health Research Unit partner lab, focusing on genomic surveillance of Antimicrobial Resistance in India.
